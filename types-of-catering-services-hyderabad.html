Types of Catering Services in Hyderabad, Explained

Veg, non-veg, buffet, live counters, plated and box meals, and how to pick the right one for your event.

Choosing between the different types of catering services in Hyderabad is often the first real decision a host makes, and it shapes your budget and how guests experience the day. Should you go pure-veg or mixed, buffet or plated, and are live counters worth it for your headcount? This guide walks through the main catering formats a Hyderabad host chooses between so you can match the service style to your event. At Jyothi Caterers we set up all of these types of catering services in Hyderabad every week, so we have written this as an honest explainer, not a sales pitch.

Broadly, the types of catering services in Hyderabad fall into two questions: what is on the plate (vegetarian or non-veg) and how it reaches your guests (buffet, live counters, plated sit-down or box meals). Most real events blend a couple of these, for example a veg buffet with one live chaat counter, or a non-veg plated lunch with box meals for staff. Understanding the types of catering services in Hyderabad before you enquire means you can describe exactly what you want and get a far more accurate quote. Below we compare each format by guest count, budget and event type, with the pros and cons that matter on the day.

Veg vs Non-Veg: The First Choice

Before you pick a service style, decide what goes on the plate. This is the most fundamental split among catering types in Hyderabad and it affects both menu and per-plate cost.

1. Vegetarian catering

Pure-veg kitchens and menus for pooja functions, naming ceremonies, Jain and satvik events, and families that prefer no onion or garlic. Usually the most affordable format and the safest default for large mixed gatherings in Hyderabad.

2. Non-veg catering

Adds chicken, mutton, fish and egg dishes, biryani and non-veg starters alongside veg options. Best for receptions, birthdays and corporate dinners where guests expect variety. Costs a little more per plate and is often served with separate veg and non-veg counters.

How It Is Served: Service Styles

Once the menu direction is set, the bigger experience decision is the service style. These are the catering formats that change how your event feels and flows.

3. Buffet service

The workhorse of Hyderabad events. Food is arranged in chafing dishes at a serving line and guests help themselves. Buffets suit almost any guest count, keep costs efficient because you need fewer serving staff, and let people choose portions. The trade-off is queues at peak time, so for larger crowds we plan multiple serving points to keep lines short.

4. Live counters and stations

Live counters are chefs cooking or assembling in front of guests: dosa, chaat, pasta, tandoor, tikka and mocktail stations are the Hyderabad favourites. They add energy and a premium feel, and they are brilliant for birthdays, receptions and get-togethers. Live counters are priced per station and per guest count, so they add to the plate cost, but even one or two counters alongside a buffet lifts the whole event.

5. Plated or sit-down service

Guests are seated and served a set menu course by course, or a traditional South Indian meal on a banana leaf. Plated service feels formal and controlled, works beautifully for intimate receptions, VIP corporate lunches and ceremonial meals, and produces almost no queueing. It needs more serving staff, so it usually costs more per head than a buffet of the same menu.

6. Tiffin, box and packed meals

Individually packed meal boxes or tiffin spreads for office lunches, training days, travel, small pooja functions and events where guests eat at their desks or on the move. This is the simplest, most portable format and often the most affordable for straightforward daytime catering.

How to Choose the Right Type for Your Event

The best format depends on your guest count, budget and the kind of event. As a rule of thumb:

  • Small home functions and office lunches often work best as a veg buffet or box meals, quick to set up and easy on budget.
  • Birthdays, get-togethers and receptions shine with a buffet plus one or two live counters for that extra buzz.
  • Weddings and large receptions usually run a full buffet with multiple counters, and sometimes a plated meal for a VIP section. See our wedding catering service and wedding catering packages for how these are structured.

What Do These Catering Types Cost?

There is no single per-plate price, because cost depends on real choices: whether you go veg or non-veg, how many items and live counters you include, your final guest count, and the venue and service style. A simple veg buffet or box-meal lunch sits at the affordable end; a non-veg spread with several starters, biryani and two or three live counters, or a fully plated meal, costs more per plate.
