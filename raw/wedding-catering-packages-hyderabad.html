How wedding catering packages are structured in Hyderabad

No single package fits both a 150-guest engagement lunch and a 700-guest reception, so caterers build packages along a few clear lines. Once you know these building blocks, comparing quotes becomes far easier.

Menu tier and buffet size are the biggest levers on your per-plate cost.
Live counters like chaat, dosa or tandoor add labour and ingredients to the package.

The four building blocks of a package

Most wedding packages are shaped by four choices working together:

Building blockHow it shapes your package
Menu tierSilver, gold or premium style tiers set the number of starters, curries, breads, rice items and desserts. Higher tiers mean a richer spread and a higher plate.
Veg vs veg-plus-non-vegPure-veg packages are usually the most affordable. Adding chicken, mutton or seafood counters lifts the rate.
Number of live countersWelcome drinks, chaat, dosa, tandoor, pasta, paan and ice-cream counters each add to labour and ingredients.
Guest slabPackages are often quoted per guest band. Larger weddings can bring the per-plate rate down through scale, while smaller functions cost a little more per head.

What drives your wedding catering rates up or down

Once the package shape is set, a few factors decide where your per-plate rate lands, and each is a lever you can use to control cost.

FactorHow it moves your rate
Menu tier and item countMore starters, curries, counters and sweets naturally lift the per-plate cost.
Veg vs non-vegPure-veg packages are the most affordable. Chicken, mutton and seafood raise the rate.
Live countersEach extra counter adds labour and ingredients, so fewer counters keep the plate lower.
Guest countLarger weddings can lower the per-head rate through scale; very small functions cost a little more per head.
Service styleBuffet is the most economical. Sit-down or table service needs more staff and lifts the price.
Venue and logisticsDistance from Boduppal, kitchen access and crockery or equipment needs can affect the final figure.
Season and datePeak wedding dates and festival weekends are busier, so booking early helps you lock a better rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much do wedding catering packages in Hyderabad cost?

There is no single fixed price. A wedding package is priced per plate based on your menu tier, veg or veg-plus-non-veg, the number of live counters, guest count and venue. We share the honest factors and indicative ranges here, then confirm your exact per-plate quote on WhatsApp once we know your details.

What is included in a typical wedding catering package?

A typical package includes welcome drinks, starters, a main-course spread of curries, rice or biryani and breads, your chosen live counters, desserts, and trained service staff with buffet setup and on-time delivery. Premium tiers add more counters and a wider menu.

Is a pure-veg wedding package cheaper than a non-veg one?

Usually, yes. Pure-veg packages tend to be the most affordable option, and Jyothi Caterers is known as a veg-focused caterer. Adding non-veg items such as chicken or mutton, or extra live counters, raises the per-plate rate. We can build a spread to match your budget either way.

Does a larger guest count lower the per-plate rate?

Often it can. Bigger weddings benefit from scale, so the per-head figure may come down compared with a small function. The menu tier and counters still matter most, so the best way to see the effect is to share your headcount and let us quote it.
