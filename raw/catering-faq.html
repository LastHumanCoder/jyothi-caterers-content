Frequently asked questions

How much does catering cost per plate in Hyderabad?

There is no fixed number. Your per-plate cost depends on veg versus non-veg, the number of items and starters, whether you add live counters, your guest count and the venue. As an indicative guide only, simpler veg menus sit at the lower end of typical Hyderabad ranges, while elaborate wedding buffets with multiple counters sit higher. We do not quote a blind figure. Is there a minimum guest count for booking?

Yes, we work best for gatherings with a reasonable minimum so we can guarantee freshness, quantity and full-service delivery. Small home functions, birthdays, corporate lunches and large weddings are all handled, but the minimum varies by menu and date. How much advance notice do you need to book catering?

The earlier the better, especially for wedding season and weekend dates which fill quickly. For large events we recommend booking a few weeks ahead so menu, staff and logistics are locked. Shorter-notice and smaller functions can sometimes be arranged, so message us with your date and we will confirm availability right away.

Yes. Jyothi Caterers is veg-focused with a strong pure-veg reputation, and we also serve non-veg menus with chicken, mutton and more.
