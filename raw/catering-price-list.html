What actually decides your catering cost in Hyderabad

There is no single "plate price" that fits a house-warming lunch, a 600-guest wedding and a small office event. Instead, a handful of clear factors move the number up or down. Once you know these, a quote stops feeling like guesswork and the numbers make sense.

Fresh, hygienic cooking done close to serving time keeps quality high across every plate.
A wider spread with more curries, chaat and sweets naturally lifts the per-plate cost.

The main cost factors, at a glance

FactorHow it moves your price
Veg vs non-vegPure-veg menus are usually the most affordable. Adding chicken, mutton or seafood raises the per-plate cost.
Number of itemsA simple meal with a few dishes costs less than a grand spread of many curries, starters, breads and sweets.
Live countersChaat, dosa, tandoor, pasta or ice-cream counters add labour and ingredients, so each one adds to the plate.
Guest countLarger events can bring the per-plate rate down through scale, while very small events cost a little more per head.
Service styleBuffet is the most economical. Sit-down or table service needs more staff and lifts the price.
Venue & logisticsDistance from Boduppal, kitchen access, and equipment or crockery needs can affect the final figure.
Season & datePeak wedding dates and festival weekends are busier, so booking early helps you lock a better rate.
