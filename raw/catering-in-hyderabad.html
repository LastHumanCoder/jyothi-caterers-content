What decides your per-plate cost

There is no single price for catering because every event is different. Instead of a fixed rate, your per-plate cost in Hyderabad depends on a few honest factors. Use the guide below to understand the range, then message us on WhatsApp for an exact quote built around your menu.

FactorHow it affects your cost
Veg vs non-vegNon-veg and mixed menus usually cost more than pure veg.
Number of items & countersMore dishes, starters and live counters raise the per-plate price.
Guest countLarger events often bring the per-plate cost down through scale.
Live countersChaat, dosa, tandoor and dessert stations add to the total.
Venue & serviceLocation, distance and buffet vs served style influence pricing.

As a broad guide only, typical Hyderabad menus range widely by event, and simple veg spreads sit lower while multi-counter non-veg menus sit higher. These are indicative ranges, so please confirm your exact quote on WhatsApp. See our full catering price list and catering packages for more detail.
