What does catering in Boduppal cost?

There is no single per-plate price for catering, and any caterer who quotes one before hearing your plan is guessing. Your real cost depends on a few honest factors:

  • Veg or non-veg: non-veg and mixed menus cost more than a pure veg spread.
  • Number of items and counters: a 6-item meal is very different from a 20-item wedding buffet with live counters.
  • Guest count: larger events often bring the per-plate rate down.
  • Live counters and specials: chaat, dosa, tandoor and dessert counters add to the plate.
  • Venue and service: staffing, distance and serving style all matter.

As a rough guide only, everyday veg catering in and around Hyderabad often sits in broad typical ranges, while elaborate wedding buffets sit higher.
