What sets the per-plate price

We do not publish fixed per-plate rates because every event is quoted on its own menu and scale. The honest factors that shape your cost are:

  • Veg vs non-veg and how many non-veg items you include.
  • Number of dishes and counters - a simple thali costs far less than a multi-counter buffet.
  • Guest count - larger events often bring a better per-plate rate.
  • Live counters such as chaat, dosa or tandoor stations.
  • Venue and service style - buffet, sit-down or packed meals.
