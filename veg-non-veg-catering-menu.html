The menu, course by course

Here is how a typical Jyothi Caterers spread is structured. Every category can be scaled up or trimmed down, and any dish can be swapped for a family favourite. Treat this as a starting frame, then we refine it together.

Welcome drinks

Guests are greeted with a refreshing welcome drink: seasonal fresh juices, buttermilk and chaas, flavoured mocktails, jaljeera or a warm option for winter functions. A small, memorable start that sets the tone.

Starters · veg

Crisp, hot and served in rounds: paneer tikka, veg manchurian, spring rolls, hara-bhara kababs, gobi and mushroom fries, and regional favourites. Choose two to five items depending on the event size.

Starters · non-veg

For your non veg catering Hyderabad menu we serve chicken tikka, chicken 65, apollo fish, kababs and pepper-fry style starters, kept warm and freshly finished so nothing arrives dry.

Main course curries

The heart of the meal: paneer and vegetable gravies, dal varieties and seasonal sabzis on the veg side, plus chicken, mutton and fish curries on the non-veg side. Mild to spicy, tuned to your family's taste.

Biryani & rice

Hyderabad expects biryani and we deliver: veg dum biryani, kaju or paneer biryani, and chicken or mutton biryani cooked in batches for consistent aroma, served alongside jeera rice, pulao and plain rice with raita and salan.

Breads (rotis & more)

Hot from the tawa or tandoor: phulka, butter naan, tandoori roti, rumali roti and pooris, refilled through the service so bread never runs cold at your buffet.

Live counters

Interactive stations add energy to weddings and receptions: chaat, dosa, pasta, tandoor, tawa and dessert counters where food is cooked and served fresh in front of guests. Add as many as your venue allows.

Desserts & sweets

Finish with gulab jamun, double ka meetha, qubani ka meetha, kheer or payasam, ice creams and seasonal sweets. Both traditional and lighter options are available for your veg catering in Hyderabad menu.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer both veg and non-veg on the same menu?

Yes. Our kitchen handles pure-veg functions and mixed events equally well. You can run a fully veg buffet, a fully non-veg spread, or combine both with clearly separated serving to respect every guest's preference.

Is the menu fixed or can I customise the dishes?

It is fully customisable. The categories on this page are a frame, not a fixed list. You choose the number of starters, curries, biryanis, breads, live counters and desserts, and we tailor each to your family's taste and budget.

Where can I see the full dish list?

The complete, printable dish list is in our catering menu brochure. For a menu built around your specific event, send your date and guest count on WhatsApp and we will share a tailored version.

How much does veg or non-veg catering cost in Hyderabad?

We quote per event rather than a single per-plate figure, because cost depends on veg versus non-veg items, the number of dishes and live counters, guest count and venue. Share your details and we confirm an exact, itemised quote quickly.

Can you add live counters to my menu?

Absolutely. Chaat, dosa, pasta, tandoor and dessert counters are popular for weddings and receptions. Live counters are added based on your guest count and the space your venue allows, and we cook and serve fresh on site.
