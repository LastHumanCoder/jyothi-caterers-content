How corporate catering pricing works in Hyderabad

There is no single fixed rate for office catering, and any caterer who quotes one before hearing your plan is guessing. Your real per-plate cost depends on a few honest factors: whether the menu is veg or non-veg, how many items and live counters you want, the guest count, the venue, and any add-ons like desserts or beverages. A simple veg working lunch sits at the lower end of typical Hyderabad ranges, while a multi-counter non-veg event menu sits higher.

Because these variables move the number so much, we do not publish flat per-plate prices here.
