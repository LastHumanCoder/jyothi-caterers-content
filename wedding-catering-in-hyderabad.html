How wedding catering rates are set

Honest answer first: there is no single fixed price for a Hyderabad wedding, and any caterer who quotes one before knowing your plan is guessing. Real wedding catering rates are usually built per plate, then multiplied by your final guest count. The factors below decide where your per-plate figure lands, and we confirm the exact number on WhatsApp once we know your details.

What shapes the rateWhy it changes the price
Veg vs non-vegNon-veg items and premium proteins cost more per plate than an all-veg menu.
Number of itemsA 15-item menu costs more per plate than a lean, curated 8-item menu.
Live countersEach live counter adds chef time, equipment and ingredients on top of the base plate.
Guest countLarger counts often lower the per-plate rate but raise the total; small counts cost a little more per plate.
Venue and logisticsDistance, kitchen access and serving-staff needs at your venue affect the final quote.

We do not publish fixed per-plate prices here because every wedding menu is different. For typical current ranges and a written estimate, see our catering price list or ask on WhatsApp and we will match a plan to your budget.
