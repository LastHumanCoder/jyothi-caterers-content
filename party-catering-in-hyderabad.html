Frequently Asked Questions

How much notice do you need to cater a party in Hyderabad?

For small house parties, a couple of days is usually enough. For larger birthdays, naming ceremonies or 150-plus gatherings, we recommend booking about a week ahead so we can confirm the menu, staff and live counters. Short-notice orders are often possible, so message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what we can arrange.

Do you offer both veg and non-veg party menus?

Yes. We are a veg-focused kitchen and also cater non-veg. You can choose a pure-veg spread, a fully non-veg menu, or a mix with separate serving to keep everyone comfortable. Share your preference and guest mix and we will build a balanced menu.

What is the minimum guest count for party catering?

We cater intimate home parties from around 20 guests all the way up to 300-plus celebrations. Smaller parties can use drop-off buffet trays, while larger events get full on-site setup with staff and live counters. Tell us your headcount for the right recommendation.

Can you set up live counters for a birthday or get-together?

Absolutely. Live chaat, dosa, pasta and mocktail counters are popular for birthdays and get-togethers and add a lot of energy to the party. Live counters are quoted based on the number of stations and your guest count, so add them to your WhatsApp message and we will include them in the quote.

How do I get an exact price for my party?

Because cost depends on veg or non-veg, number of items, live counters, guest count and venue, we quote each party individually rather than posting a fixed per-plate rate. Send your date, guest count, location and any menu ideas on WhatsApp and we will reply with a clear, itemised quote.
